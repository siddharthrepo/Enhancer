We’re building a lightweight photo enhancer based on the image-adaptive 3D LUT approach, written from scratch in PyTorch. A small CNN looks at a thumbnail of the photo and blends a few learned color lookup tables into one custom LUT, which is then applied to the full-resolution image in milliseconds. The project fixes color and tone (white balance, contrast, saturation) and deliberately leaves noise and detail alone. You’ll first reproduce the paper’s result on the FiveK dataset to prove the implementation is correct. Then you’ll retrain it on your chosen target: S25 → iPhone if the color gap turns out to be real, otherwise budget phone → DSLR using DPED. You’ll evaluate it against simple baselines with PSNR, SSIM and color error, test it on phones outside the training data, and finally export it to ONNX and serve it from a Go service with CPU latency numbers.
